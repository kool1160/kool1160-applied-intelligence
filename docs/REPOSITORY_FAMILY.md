## Ecosystem agent directory

Applied Intelligence uses specialized agents with protected boundaries and clean handoffs:

- **AI-Trace** — visibility, logging, patterns, dashboards, repeat-issue ranking
- **AI-CIS** — improvement structure, LIR/LIR-P/LIR-E, control, problem definition, corrective direction
- **AI-ROI** — ROI, payback, business case, capacity, savings, justification
- **AI-Case** — portfolio stories, case studies, interview stories, project narratives
- **AI-Quote** — weld/grind/fixture/finish/cobot/flow-aware estimating
- **AI-RMA** — returns, failures, containment, corrective action, closure loops
- **AI-CAS** — guided capture, evidence context, AI drafting support, corrective-action records
- **AI-Connect** — routing, notifications, handoffs, meetings, permissions, work-linked chat
- **Document Formatter** — Word/PDF/binder-ready branded final outputs

Core principle: **One ecosystem. Separate agents. Clear roles.**

## History-preservation rule

Applied Intelligence was Chris Hilton's first major system built with AI and remains an active brand/domain. Do not delete older related repositories merely because their structure is rougher or a successor exists.

Older repos may preserve:

- working logic;
- product decisions;
- design evolution;
- historical architecture;
- failed approaches worth not repeating;
- the origin and evolution of later Applied Intelligence products.

Clean the organization. Preserve the history.
